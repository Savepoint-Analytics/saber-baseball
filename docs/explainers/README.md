# Explainers

Plain-language pieces on what the models are trying to capture and why, written for
people who know baseball rather than statistics. They exist because a number nobody
understands is a number nobody should act on.

| | |
|---|---|
| [Why Innings Hang Together](01-why-innings-hang-together.md) | Games aren't nine independent innings, and what our simulator loses by pretending they are. |
| [The Big Inning](02-the-big-inning.md) | How rallies snowball, what the model gets right about them, and what it still misses. |
| [Home Field Advantage Is Real, and Smaller Than You Think](03-home-field-advantage.md) | Where 53% comes from, and the advantage hiding in the rulebook. |
| [Cold Nights and Thin Air](04-weather-and-ballparks.md) | Temperature, altitude and wind — and why they explain less than you'd guess. |
| [When a Pitcher Has It](05-when-a-pitcher-has-it.md) | What "he didn't have his good stuff" looks like in the numbers, and how much of it is real. |
| [The Same Trick, Four Times](06-the-same-trick-four-times.md) | The method behind every adjustment: direction, mean preservation, and measuring instead of tuning. |

The technical counterpart to these is [`../methodology.md`](../methodology.md), which
carries the validation numbers, the failures, and the code paths.
